Professional Summary

I lead the AI team at Calici Co., Ltd., working at the intersection of machine learning and computational drug discovery. My research covers molecular property and ADMET prediction under limited data, structure-based modelling of biomolecular complexes, and the computational design of peptide and antibody therapeutics. I am preparing to pursue doctoral research in this field.
